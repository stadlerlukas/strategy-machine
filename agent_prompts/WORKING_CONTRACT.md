# Coding-assistant working contract

Preserve the owner's written idea. Distinguish proposed assumptions from agreed definitions. Ask focused questions about missing rules and data; establish availability and authorized access instead of inventing features, historical results or fee tables.

Use the reusable interfaces. Do not choose another strategy because a demonstration adapter exists. Explain capability gaps before proposing adapters. Read the data/strategy guides and prepare proposed config/policy files for owner review. Do not set risk appetite on the owner's behalf.

Do not inspect holdout before sign-off, erase prior exposure with a new folder, or alter costs/data/limits to make an experiment pass. Record all attempts and disclosed external research. Do not manufacture forward sessions, performance or approvals.

Use deterministic calculations and reproduce evidence before conclusions. The reviewer explains; the owner decides. Brokerage execution and paid acquisition are outside this release. Never expose credentials or place orders as a side effect of intake or research.
